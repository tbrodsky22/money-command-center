Privacy Policy

Last updated September 13, 2026

Money Command Center is designed to help users understand and organize their personal financial information. This policy describes the categories of data the service may process and the controls available to users.

Information processed

Account information may include name, email address, encrypted password credentials, financial profile information entered by the user, budgets, debts, assets, goals and saved purchase scenarios. When a user connects a financial institution through Plaid, the service may process account metadata, balances and transaction information returned by Plaid. Money Command Center does not ask users to provide their bank username or password directly.

How information is used

Information is used to provide dashboards, transaction analysis, budgeting, cash-flow forecasts, affordability scenarios, Money Watch alerts, debt and goal planning, and Money Guide responses. Detailed transaction context is used for Money Guide when needed for a transaction-related request rather than being included in every conversation by default.

Service providers

The service currently relies on infrastructure and service providers including Railway for application hosting and database infrastructure, Plaid for supported financial-account connectivity, and OpenAI for Money Guide features. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy commitments.

Security

The application uses encrypted transport, encrypted storage of Plaid access tokens, httpOnly secure session cookies, request rate limits, origin checks, data-access ownership checks and signed Plaid webhook verification at the application edge. No security system can guarantee absolute protection.

Your controls

From the Security & Privacy area of the application, users can export their saved data, disconnect linked banks and delete their account. Disconnecting a bank attempts to revoke the associated Plaid Item and removes imported account and transaction data for that connection. Deleting an account removes the user's Money Command Center records from the application database, subject to limited records that may need to be retained for security, legal or operational obligations.

Financial information and AI

Money Guide is an educational decision-support feature and is not a substitute for a licensed financial, tax, legal, insurance or investment professional. Users should avoid entering information that is not needed for the feature they are using.

Children

The service is intended for adults and is not designed for children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the product, providers and legal obligations change. Material changes should be reflected by updating this page and its revision date.
